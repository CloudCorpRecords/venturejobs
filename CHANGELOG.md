# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-28)

- First release of the venturejobs rebuild: a local forwarding client for the hosted jobs service covering recently funded startups.
- All 14 captured tools served locally for tools/list and forwarded on call: job search and detail, application-form questions, company research, an application tracker, company lists, saved searches with alerts, and account lookup.
- Streamable-HTTP JSON-RPC forwarding with session reuse, optional bearer key forwarding from the environment, and upstream results returned unchanged.
